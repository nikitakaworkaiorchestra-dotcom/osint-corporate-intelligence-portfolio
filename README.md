## Method

1. **Scope the question** — define what must be established and to what standard.
2. **Collect across source types** — registries, filings, imagery, news, sanctions
   lists, industrial indicators.
3. **Resolve entities** — match people and companies across jurisdictions where
   transliteration and legal-entity forms diverge.
4. **Test for contradictions** — flag where sources disagree rather than
   smoothing over the conflict.
5. **Report with traceability** — state the finding, the evidence, and what
   remains unknown.
